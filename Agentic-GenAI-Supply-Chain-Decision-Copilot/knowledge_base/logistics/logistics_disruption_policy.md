## 1. Freight Disruption Contingency
Major logistics disruptions (port closures, customs blockages, weather severe events) trigger immediate supply chain re-routing protocols.
- Ocean freight to air freight conversion authorized for high-margin electronics SKUs.
- Multi-hub inventory balancing activated to prevent regional stockouts.

## 1. Inter-Warehouse Transfer Criteria
When a regional warehouse faces a projected stockout ($CRITICAL$ risk) and another distribution center holds stock in excess of its safety stock buffer ($Current > ROP + SS$), an internal warehouse transfer is executed.
- Minimum transfer quantity: 20 units.
- Transfer transit time target: 2 business days.

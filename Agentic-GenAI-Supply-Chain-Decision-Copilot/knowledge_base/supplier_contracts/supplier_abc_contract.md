# Master Procurement Agreement — Supplier ABC

**Document Status**: Synthetic / Demonstration Project Document  
**Document Type**: Supplier Contract  
**Supplier**: Supplier ABC (SUP-001)  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2024  
**Version**: 1.0  

---

## 1. Scope & Delivery Terms
Supplier ABC agrees to provide electronic components, microcontrollers, and sensor assemblies to SupplyChain AI distribution centers.
- **Standard Lead Time**: 7 calendar days from purchase order confirmation date.
- **FOB Point**: Origin (Supplier Warehouse).
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)**: 100 units per order line item.

---

## 2. Late Delivery Policy & Delay Penalties
Timely delivery is essential to buyer operations.
- **Delays of 1 to 2 calendar days**: Supplier ABC must provide written notice to Procurement within 24 hours of delay identification. No financial penalty applies.
- **Delays of 3 to 6 calendar days**: Buyer receives a 2% price credit on the delayed purchase order line item for each day overdue beyond Day 2.
- **Delays exceeding 7 calendar days**: Constitutes a material breach. Buyer reserves the right to cancel the purchase order without cancellation penalty and issue an emergency purchase order to an alternative vendor.

---

## 3. Expedited Shipping Terms
If a delivery delay exceeds 3 calendar days due to Supplier ABC operational bottlenecks, Supplier ABC shall pay for expedited air freight shipping upon buyer's request to minimize assembly line downtime.

---

## 4. Quality Standards & Defect Warranty
All delivered products must maintain a minimum 98% defect-free quality acceptance rate upon initial warehouse inspection.
- Defective items exceeding 2% of shipment lot shall be replaced by Supplier ABC within 5 business days at supplier expense.

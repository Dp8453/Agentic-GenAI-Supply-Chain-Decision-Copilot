## 1. Formal Dispute Protocol
When a supplier repeatedly breaches lead-time contracts or quality thresholds, Procurement initiates formal vendor dispute proceedings.
- Written Notice of Non-Compliance issued within 3 business days of breach.
- Corrective Action Plan (CAP) must be submitted by vendor within 7 calendar days.

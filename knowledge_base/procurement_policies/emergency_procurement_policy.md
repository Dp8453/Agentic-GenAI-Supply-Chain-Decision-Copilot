# Emergency Procurement Operating Procedure

**Document Status**: Synthetic / Demonstration Project Policy  
**Document Type**: Procurement Policy  
**Effective Date**: February 1, 2024  
**Version**: 1.0  

---

## 1. Emergency Procurement Conditions
Emergency Procurement protocol may be initiated under any of the following conditions:
1. Projected inventory trajectory reaches zero before the arrival of a scheduled replenishment purchase order.
2. A primary vendor announces an unscheduled factory shutdown or force majeure event exceeding 5 calendar days.
3. Customer demand spikes exceed 200% of forecasted 14-day rolling average.

---

## 2. Emergency Execution Protocol
- Expedited secondary vendor sourcing is authorized with up to a 20% price premium over standard contract unit cost.
- Inter-warehouse expedited stock transfers are executed automatically if another facility holds stock above its safety threshold.

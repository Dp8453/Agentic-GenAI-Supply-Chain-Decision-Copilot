# Corporate Procurement & Purchasing Guidelines

**Document Status**: Synthetic / Demonstration Project Policy  
**Document Type**: Procurement Policy  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2024  
**Version**: 2.0  

---

## 1. Standard Purchasing Workflow
All replenishment purchase orders must originate from certified inventory requirements or reorder point alerts generated by the Inventory Decision Engine.
- Orders under $10,000 require Inventory Manager approval.
- Orders between $10,000 and $50,000 require Director of Procurement approval.
- Orders exceeding $50,000 require VP of Supply Chain authorization.

---

## 2. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) Compliance
Purchasing agents must respect supplier Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ). If a calculated deficit is less than the supplier's MOQ, the order quantity must be rounded up to the MOQ or integer multiples thereof.

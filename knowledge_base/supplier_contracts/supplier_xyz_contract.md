## 1. Supply Agreement & Lead Times
Supplier XYZ provides industrial hardware components, enclosures, and structural fittings.
- **Promised Lead Time**: 10 calendar days.
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)**: 200 units per line item.
- **Delivery Mode**: Standard Ground Freight.

---

## 2. Punctuality & Delay Escrow
- **Minor Delays (1-3 days)**: Permitted up to twice per calendar quarter without contract penalty.
- **Extended Delays (4-7 days)**: Liquidated damages equal to 1.5% of PO value per day overdue.
- **Severe Delays (>7 days)**: Buyer may initiate inter-warehouse transfer or re-allocate order volumes to secondary pre-approved suppliers.

---

## 3. Quality & Acceptance Terms
- Inspection threshold: 95% defect-free acceptance.
- Returns for non-conforming items must be credited within 10 business days.

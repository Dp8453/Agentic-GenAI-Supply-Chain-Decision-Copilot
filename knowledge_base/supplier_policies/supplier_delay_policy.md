## 1. Delay Classification & Escalation Matrix
SupplyChain AI classifies supplier shipment delays into four distinct operational escalation tiers:

- **Tier 1 (Delay < 2 calendar days)**: Status classified as `MONITOR`. Purchasing agent conducts automated tracking check. No vendor penalty.
- **Tier 2 (Delay 2 to 5 calendar days)**: Status classified as `SUPPLIER_FOLLOWUP`. Vendor Account Manager receives automated inquiry requesting revised delivery schedule.
- **Tier 3 (Delay 5 to 7 calendar days)**: Status classified as `PROCUREMENT_ESCALATION`. Procurement Lead reviews contract penalty terms, credit clauses, and secondary supplier options.
- **Tier 4 (Delay > 7 calendar days)**: Status classified as `EMERGENCY_REVIEW`. Executive escalation initiated; buyer may issue PO cancellation and trigger emergency secondary procurement.

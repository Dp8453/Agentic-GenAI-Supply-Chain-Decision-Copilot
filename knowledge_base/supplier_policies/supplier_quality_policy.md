## 1. Quality Acceptance Thresholds
All incoming inventory lots are sampled and inspected upon arrival at distribution hubs.
- Minimum lot acceptance threshold: 97.5% defect-free.
- Vendors falling below 90% quality score for two consecutive months are placed on `UNDER_REVIEW` contract status.
